# Open Source Governance

This repository contains research, white papers, and structural frameworks for **resilient, transparent, and baseline-preserving governance**. All work is openly published for adaptation, reuse, and critical exploration.

## Overview

Open Source Governance reframes policy creation as **structural engineering**, not ideology.  
It focuses on solutions generated **from the problem space**—by those living with the system's effects—rather than relying on captured institutions.  

Key principles:
- **Baseline Preservation:** Ensure that policies and systems can revert to a safe state without social rupture.
- **Optionality & Resilience:** Maintain adaptive capacity in political, economic, and social systems.
- **Transparency & Auditability:** Make influence, funding, and algorithmic effects visible to all stakeholders.
- **Non-Partisan, Anti-Capture Design:** Policies and mechanisms operate independently of institutional or lobby group bias.

## Core Mechanisms & Research

This repository includes papers and frameworks covering:

- **Sovereign Public Banking:** Resource-backed banking that preserves baseline economic security while enabling competitive service contracts.  
- **Labour-First Tax Systems:** Ensuring essential income is untaxed, protecting survival without debt.  
- **Democracy Vouchers:** Redirecting existing voter contributions to reduce lobby group influence.  
- **Algorithmic Transparency:** Revealing digital influence in elections, including amplification and suppression mechanisms.  
- **Third-Party Campaign Disclosure:** Exposing hidden institutional influence to enable public accountability.  
- **Constituency Inversion:** Policies created from the problem space, empowering ordinary citizens to propose and implement solutions.

## Goals

This repository is designed for:
- **Policy makers & independent candidates** seeking open-source solutions.
- **Researchers & technologists** interested in algorithmic transparency, structural governance, and anti-capture economics.
- **Civic-minded citizens** exploring how to generate resilient, transparent policies from lived experience.

All work is **free to use, adapt, and redistribute**. The author intentionally operates **from the problem space**, outside institutional structures, demonstrating that actionable solutions do not require formal authority or academic credentials.
